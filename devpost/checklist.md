# Build Checklist

Build mode: fast

Verification approach: API checks with `curl` against local Ollama, plus a throwaway Playwright script (`playwright-core` driving the installed Chrome, kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repo) that loads `http://localhost:8000` and asserts on the real page with the real model. No test framework is added to the project.

## Slices

- [x] **1. Paste a pitch and the three judges deliver verdicts, one at a time**
  Becomes usable: The stage page with all three judges waiting. You paste a pitch, press "Submit to the panel", and Vera, Marco and Ingrid reveal left to right, each with a score, a one-line reason and one fix, from the real local model.
  Why now: It's the riskiest part (does `/api/chat` honour the schema, does CORS allow `localhost:8000`) and the heart of the kernel (three conflicting judges), so both get proven first. Scaffolding and fonts come along because this slice needs somewhere to render.
  PRD ref: `prd.md > The Core Journey` (steps 1–4), `prd.md > Screens and Layout`, `prd.md > The Judges`, `prd.md > Deliberation and Reveal`
  Spec ref: `spec.md > Page Shell (index.html)`, `spec.md > Judge Definitions (judges.js)`, `spec.md > Judge Call (callJudge in app.js)`, `spec.md > Verdict Validation (validateVerdict in app.js)`, `spec.md > Round Controller (app.js)`, `spec.md > Judge Card Rendering (app.js + style.css)`, `spec.md > Look and Feel`, `spec.md > Ollama /api/chat (local)`, `spec.md > File Structure`
  Build: First, curl `/api/chat` with the schema to confirm the response shape. Then create `index.html`, `style.css` (stage theme, cards, Reading… spinner, placeholder portraits with initials), `judges.js` (the three personas, seeds, `buildMessages` without the previous note for now), and `app.js` (`callJudge`, `validateVerdict`, a sequential `submitRound`/`runJudge`, card states waiting/reading/done, box lock and "Panel is deliberating…"). Self-host the Bebas Neue and Inter woff2 files with their OFL licences in `assets/fonts/`. Add `.gitignore` entries and a minimal README with the start command.
  Verify (mechanical): The curl returns JSON whose `message.content` parses to `{score:int, reason, fix}`. With `python -m http.server 8000` running, the Playwright script loads the page, finds 3 cards showing "Waiting for your pitch…", submits a sample pitch, sees all cards in Reading… and the button disabled, then sees cards fill in order engineer → marketer → investor, each with an integer 0–100, a non-empty reason and a non-empty fix. No console errors and no CORS failure.
  Learner check: Run `python -m http.server 8000`, open http://localhost:8000, paste a real pitch and submit. Watch the three judges reveal one by one. Do they sound like three different people with different priorities, and does the stage feel like the panel you pictured?
  Commit: `Add judge panel with sequential local-model verdicts`

  Why now: This is the other half of the kernel, "a number that moves", and the prompts and previous-verdict note need your feedback before the rest of the build depends on them. The early hands-on checkpoint comes right after this slice.
  PRD ref: `prd.md > The Core Journey` (steps 5–7), `prd.md > Total Badge and Verdict`, `prd.md > Resubmit and Score Movement`

  Verify (mechanical): Run the Playwright script with a deliberately weak pitch, then a rewrite that applies the investor's fix. - [x] **3. The panel copes with bad input and failures**
  Becomes usable: Submit stays disabled under 20 characters, the counter warns past 800 and 1500 (sending only the first 1500 characters), a failed judge shows "The judge left the room — retry" with its own retry button, and if all three fail the banner asks "Can't reach the local model.   Why now: The happy path works by this point. These are the PRD's what-ifs, and they touch the same round controller, so they go before persistence starts saving its results.
  PRD ref: `prd.md > Pitch Box and Submit`, `prd.md > Per-Judge Retry`, `prd.md > States and Boundaries`
  Spec ref: `spec.md > Pitch Box and Counter (app.js)`, `spec.md > Round Controller (app.js)`, `spec.md > All-Fail Banner (index.html + app.js)`, `spec.md > Verdict Validation (validateVerdict in app.js)`, `spec.md > Important Failure Modes`
  Build: Add the counter with its states and the 1500-character cut in `buildMessages`, the 20-character Submit gate, the 60 s `AbortController` timeout, the failed card state with `retryJudge`, the badge "Waiting for N judges" after a failure, and banner show/hide.
  Verify (mechanical): The Playwright script checks four things. Submit is disabled at 19 characters and enabled at 20. The counter class is amber at 801 characters and red at 1501, with the warning text. One judge is forced to fail by intercepting its request with an error: only that card shows the retry, the badge is held, and retrying fills it in and resolves the badge. All requests are blocked to simulate Ollama being down: the banner and three failed cards appear, and the banner hides after a successful retry. Node checks confirm `validateVerdict` rejects a score of 101, 42.5, "58", an empty reason, and a missing fix.
  Learner check: Quit Ollama and submit. You should see the banner and three "left the room" cards. Start Ollama again and hit retry on each card. Then paste a very long pitch and watch the counter change colour.

  Why now: These are the two items in the agreed cut order (animation first, then persistence), so they go last, where dropping them costs nothing that came before.

  Verify (mechanical): The Playwright script checks three things. After a complete round, a reload shows the same pitch, scores, reasons and deltas. It also samples the badge text during the count-up and sees intermediate values that end at the final total.
  Learner check: Do a round, resubmit, then reload the page. Everything should still be there, arrows included. Press "New pitch" and confirm you're back to the waiting panel.

## Revisions
- Pure-function checks (total, delta, and later `validateVerdict`) run in the browser by importing the live `app.js` module, not in Node — `app.js` builds the panel on load, so it can't be imported in Node without a DOM; the module cache means the import adds no side effects.
- Judge prompts reworked at the slice-2 checkpoint — learner testing showed anchoring (marketer said the audience was missing when the new pitch named it), scores clustering at 35/45, polite tone, and the engineer inventing requirements. Now: read fresh, previous score only as reference, no citing stated things as missing, quote the weakest sentence, banded rubric stated in the reason, blunt one-sentence reason and fix, engineer judges only what is claimed. Iterating against the test pair also showed the model copying `<placeholder>` templates literally and answering "no change needed", so the prompt uses exact band labels and a worked example, and requires a fix.
- Evidence-first verdict schema (`evidence` → `band` → `reason` → `score` → `fix`), `MODEL` constant moved to `judges.js`, and one automatic retry with `seed + 1` on an unusable answer — on the learner's real pitch pair, 14B claimed the stated audience was unspecified and pinned every score to its band floor; evidence-first was the only variant that found the audience, but sometimes returned an empty reason, hence the retry. A fixed seed means a same-seed retry would repeat the answer. Model choice resolved below.
- During this checkpoint Ollama slowed to ~0.09 tokens/s because the GPU was nearly full (23.1/24.5 GB with other GPU apps running); this was environmental, not an app bug. Checks that don't need model quality now use a mocked `/api/chat` (Playwright `route`), so they don't depend on GPU state.
- Model switched to `qwen2.5:32b` — compared with 14B on the recipe pair, the learner's real pair and the marketer test. Only 32B passed the marketer test (it stopped calling a stated audience missing, and the investor stayed low); about 2.7 s per judge vs 1.4 s for 14B.
- Requests now send `num_ctx: 4096` — at Ollama's default 32k context, 32B loaded at 28 GB, split 21% CPU / 79% GPU, and took 21–58 s per judge (close to the 60 s timeout). At 4096 it is 20 GB, 100% GPU.
- Validation also rejects a reason with an empty quote (`""` / `“”`), with the same one retry — 14B produced `Band 0-30 (absent): "" …`. An empty `evidence` field is still allowed, because that is how a judge signals "absent".
- Engineer prompt: a pitch that says what the product does can't be banded 0–30 — 32B dropped the engineer 45 → 20 on the improved recipe rewrite; now 50 → 50.
- Demo story verified on 32B in the real UI: the learner's three rounds score 30 → 48 → 75 (Would advance); the learner reproduced it at the hands-on checkpoint.
- Portraits drawn as flat-shaded SVG and rendered to 512×512 PNG instead of generated with Codex — no image model was available in the build session. Sources and the render script are in `assets/portraits/src/`.
- Final verification (32B, 100% GPU, `num_ctx` 4096): mocked checks s3 (18), s4 (8), retry (7) pass; real-model s1 (13), s2 (38) and the 3-round story pass; CORS allows `http://localhost:8000`. s2 had been reading the badge mid count-up (a test written before slice 4), so it now waits for the animation. Fixed seed + temperature 0.2 is not bit-exact on the GPU: the same recipe pitch gave the engineer 50 in one run and 45 in another. The demo story reproduced 30 → 48 → 75 in all four runs.
- Final-review prompt changes: "The fix is ONE sentence to add or change, never a rewrite of the pitch", a 300-character fix cap (one retry), the marketer's "name the well-known product" rule, and `「」` added to the empty-quote check. An explicit "Answer in the language of the pitch" line was tested and dropped: in every placement, the engineer's seed (101) moved the demo's round 3 from 75 to 50 (total 67, Would not advance). Rewriting the round-3 pitch fixed the engineer but dropped the marketer to 45. Without the line, the story holds at 30 → 48 → 75 and Japanese pitches still get Japanese verdicts (one reason came back in English after the quote).
- Japanese UI moved from Deferred into the build at the learner's request, kept small: one `STRINGS` object with `t()`, `data-i18n` on static text, `data-label` for the "Fix:" prefix (replacing CSS `content` strings), Japanese system-font fallback, and the toggle moved above the title on phones (it overlapped the title at 390 px). Verified with mocked verdicts in EN and JA at 1200 px and 390 px: no overflow in the done, previous-round, failed or banner states; English strings unchanged; requests identical in both languages; the choice persists across reloads. Real-model story re-verified afterwards: 30 → 48 → 75.
- Input limits raised after shipping prep: soft warning at 3000 characters, hard cut at 6000 (was 800 / 1500), and `num_ctx` 4096 → 6144. Measured with the real engineer prompt: a 6000-character pitch is ~2,000 prompt tokens in English and ~4,500 in Japanese, which 4096 cannot hold. 8192 was tried first: `ollama ps` said 100% GPU, but Windows spilled ~1 GB of it into shared system memory (VRAM was full, with the desktop compositor holding 4.8 GB), generation fell from 43 to 12 tokens/s, and the demo story came back as 28 → 57 → 75 with 59–110 s rounds. At 6144 (the agreed fallback) the spill is ~260 MB, generation is ~22 tokens/s, and the story is unchanged at 30 → 48 → 75 (Would advance), with 38–50 s rounds (13–16 s per judge, inside the 60 s timeout). At 4096 there is no spill and rounds take 9–11 s, but a long Japanese pitch would be cut off.
- Verdict language selector added after shipping prep: "Verdicts in" Auto / English / 日本語 next to the EN / JA toggle, default Auto, saved in `localStorage` (`aiJudges.verdictLang`). Auto adds nothing to the prompts, so the demo story is untouched; English / 日本語 append "Answer in English." / "日本語で答えてください。" to every judge prompt. Verified with mocked Ollama: default Auto, exact prompt suffixes for all three judges, persistence across reload, locked during a round, no overlap or horizontal scroll at 1200 / 390 px in both UI languages; existing checks still pass.
- Verdict language selector fix: with the first version ("Answer in English." / "日本語で答えてください。" appended as a bare last line) the learner saw 日本語 verdicts come back word for word identical to the English round. The server log shows the browser had fetched the new code and the mocked check confirmed the line was sent, so most likely the model ignored a bare trailing line. The line is now a hard rule as the last paragraph: "Write reason and fix in Japanese/English. Keep the quoted evidence in its original language." Real-model check with the request logged: switching English → 日本語 on the unchanged round-3 pitch gave a fresh verdict with Japanese reasons and fixes and English quotes; the 日本語 story is 28 → 47 → 75 (Would advance), the English story 28 → 47 → 65. Auto still sends the original prompts, so the 30 → 48 → 75 demo is untouched.
- **v1.1 submission review (after shipping).** Added the optional "Attach your submission" section: four labelled documents (load `.md`/`.txt` in the browser), editable judging criteria pre-filled verbatim from Devpost, a 12,000-character combined limit, `criterion` + `source` in the verdict and a "Criterion · Source" line on the cards. Review mode is on only when a document has text, so the pitch-only request is byte-identical to v1 (golden check of all 9 demo-story requests, Auto and English). Additions during the build: each judge is pinned to a home criterion when the three default criteria are present (left free, all three picked Presentation); `source` must be a document sent and the evidence must be in it verbatim (whitespace-normalized), else one retry then "left the room"; documents are wrapped in `=== name ===` markers and `source`/`criterion` are schema enums; and `evidence` now comes before `source`, because source-first mislabelled story quotes as "Pitch". Real-model run with the Project story: Design · Project story 75 / Presentation · Project story 75 / Innovation/Idea · Pitch 75 → 75, Would advance, no retries, 15–17 s per round.
- **Language selector:** picking the pitch's own language now adds nothing (same prompt as Auto); only a different language adds the hard rule.
- **Context and speed:** the asked-for `num_ctx` 16384 didn't fit (62/65 layers on the GPU, >5 min per judge), nor did 12288 with the default KV cache (shared-memory spill, >5 min). With `OLLAMA_FLASH_ATTENTION=1` and `OLLAMA_KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0`, 12288 is 100% on the GPU with no spill, ~38 tokens/s. That also fixed the slow pitch rounds: at 6144 with the f16 cache, rounds took ~37 s (21 tokens/s, ~260 MB spill); the 84–108 s rounds seen earlier added model reloads after idle and a second client queuing. With q8_0, pitch rounds are back to 9–12 s. The demo story under q8_0: 30 → 48 → 75 (Would advance) on 2 of 3 runs; the first run after a model load gave the engineer 45 instead of 50 (28 → 47 → 75), the same wobble seen before the change.
- **Submission review, criterion only:** the investor scored Innovation/Idea on money because her persona priority outweighed the criteria. Review mode now keeps only each judge's identity line and adds "your criterion is the ONLY thing you score" plus a question per pinned criterion. On the story, the panel went from 75 to 55, with Vera and Ingrid each at 45.
- **Verdict language enforced:** with English or 日本語 selected, a reason or fix in the wrong language (quotes ignored) is retried once with seed + 1.
- **"New pitch" resets submission review too:** it used to clear only the pitch and the round, leaving the attachments and criteria behind. It now also empties the four attachments, restores the default labels and criteria, and removes their saved copy.
- **v1.2.0, file attachments:** each slot loads .md/.txt/.pdf/.docx/.pptx/.xlsx and extracts the text in the browser (pdf.js, JSZip and SheetJS vendored under `vendor/`, loaded on demand). Also added a version footer, CHANGELOG.md and release tags (v1.0.0 to v1.2.0).
