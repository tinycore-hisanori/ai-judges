AI Judges: a pitch-critique tool for people who enter hackathons. Paste your 3–5 line pitch; three judges with conflicting priorities — a skeptical engineer, a marketer asking who actually uses it, an investor asking how it makes money — each reject it with a score and one concrete fix. Rewrite and resubmit; watch the same panel change its mind. It runs fully on your PC via Ollama, so pitches never leave your machine. Free and open source for individuals; a $9/month team tier adds a shared judge panel tuned to a company's own pitch criteria. Built by an indie dev who enters several hackathons a month and uses it on every submission.
